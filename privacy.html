Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

The short version

This website does not use cookies, does not ask you for personal information, and does not sell or share anything about you. We count visits so we know whether the site is useful, and that is all.

Visit counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see how many people visit and which links they click. For each page view it records:

  • The page you viewed and the site that sent you here, if any
  • Your browser, operating system and screen size
  • Your country, worked out from your IP address

GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. The data is only ever seen in totals, never as a profile of one person. You can read GoatCounter’s privacy policy.

Booking a call

The “Book a call” links take you to Calendly, a separate scheduling service. Anything you enter there, such as your name and email, is handled under Calendly’s privacy notice and shared with us so we can meet with you.

Fonts

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them, which means Google receives your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to work with you. We don’t add you to any mailing list.
