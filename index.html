Straightforward answers

Is it appropriate for a school to contract with an outside LLC?

Yes. Schools regularly contract with outside providers such as auditors, legal counsel and IT firms, and this is no different. Our engagement is a standard vendor relationship, not an employment arrangement. Schools should confirm alignment with their conference treasurer, which we are happy to support.

How is sensitive student and financial data protected?

We use secure file transfer, access controls and FERPA-conscious data handling. We work inside the same systems your school already uses, which limits data exposure, and we are happy to sign a data processing or confidentiality agreement.

Will GCAS auditors be comfortable with this arrangement?

Our documentation practices are designed with GCAS audit readiness in mind. We keep clean, organized records throughout the year rather than scrambling at audit time, and we know what auditors look for.

What systems do you work in?

We work natively in AASI.NET and FACTS, the core platforms used across Adventist K–12 schools, and we run payables through Ramp. If your school uses additional tools, we adapt.

Can you work with our existing on-site staff?

Absolutely. Accounting Support is designed for schools that have a part-time bookkeeper or office manager on site. We handle the complex work, such as reconciliations, close and reporting, while coordinating with your team.

Do you handle payroll?

No. Faculty and staff payroll is typically managed through the conference, and student payroll stays with your school. We keep the books those payrolls post to.
