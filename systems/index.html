Straightforward answers

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with what you already pay for, such as Excel, Microsoft 365 and your accounting system. If a new tool would clearly pay for itself, we’ll show you the numbers first.

Our team is spread across several states. Does that matter?

No. Interviews and walkthroughs happen by video with screen sharing, so we see exactly how the work gets done, wherever your people are.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything lives in your accounts and comes with plain-language documentation, so your team isn’t dependent on us to keep it running.

Is this about replacing people?

No. It’s about giving your best people their time back from repetitive work, so they can spend it on the parts of the job that need their judgment.

How is it priced?

Fixed prices, not hourly billing. The first project and the diagnostic are each a set fee with a defined deliverable. Ongoing support is a flat monthly retainer.

Do you also do accounting?

Yes. Accounting is where we started, and we run the books for schools. That background is why we look at every process through the numbers it produces. See our school services.
